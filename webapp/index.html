Academic & Operations Guide

5 Core Inventory Features

Mathematical formulations, data structures, and live system locations.

1

Maintain Stock & Sales Records

Real-Time Ledger

Maintains real-time stock quantities across 4 boutique sectors. POS checkouts decrement stock and increment a dedicated todaysSales counter. Calling Next Day triggers closeDay(): appends today's sales into a rolling 14-day history queue (bounded at 14 days) and resets the day accumulator.

Mechanism & Bounds: todaysSales += qty; closeDay() → max 14 entries
Atomic Undo: Capped 20-entry LIFO transaction stack restores both shelf stock and today's sales.
2

Calculate Average Daily Sales (ADS)

14-Day Linear WMA

Predicts velocity using a 14-day Linear Weighted Moving Average. Day 1 (oldest) has weight 1; Day 14 (yesterday) has weight 14. Total weights sum to 105. Recent sales carry 14× more statistical weight than older days.

Formula: ADS = Σ(i × Sales[i]) / 105  (where i = 1..14)
3

Estimate Days Until Stock Runs Out

Velocity & Surge

Forecasts the exhaustion timeline before shelves reach zero. Factors in the dynamic footfall multiplier (1.0× to 2.5× for weekend and holiday rushes). Safe against zero-sales products (marked infinite safe).

Formula: Days Left = Current Stock / (ADS × Multiplier)
4

Generate Reorder Suggestions

ROP & Safety Buffer

Triggers automatic replenishment when shelf stock breaches the Reorder Point (ROP). Computes the economic restock quantity to cover supplier lead time delay plus an additional 7-day safety buffer.

Formulas: ROP = (ADS × Lead Time) + Safety Stock
Suggested Qty = (Lead Time + 7) × ADS
5

Rank Products by Urgency

PriorityQueue Min-Heap

Uses a Java PriorityQueue (Min-Heap) ordered by fewest days until stockout. Immediately segments all inventory items into 3 operational risk tiers:

🔴 CRITICAL: ≤ 2 days of stock remaining
🟡 WARNING: ≤ 5 days until reorder point
🟢 SAFE: > 5 days healthy buffer
